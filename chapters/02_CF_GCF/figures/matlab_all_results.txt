Interpretation:
  Perfect coherence stays near 1 even for K=0 because its spectrum
  is concentrated exactly at DC.
  A smooth phase ramp can have CF near 0 because its spectral peak
  moved from DC to a neighboring low-frequency bin.
  GCF with a small K can recognize that low-order structure.
  Increasing K too far is not automatically better: eventually more
  incoherent / high-frequency energy is also admitted.
