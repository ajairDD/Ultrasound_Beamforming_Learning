--- demo_das_failure_modes ---
=== DAS failure-mode teaching demo ===

[A] Two-target resolution
Close separation = 0.400 mm
Wide  separation = 1.200 mm

[B] Strong / weak target
Weak target amplitude = 0.030 = -30.46 dB relative amplitude

[C] Independent noise
M = 64 coherent channels
Ideal normalized-DAS SNR gain for independent noise ~= 10log10(M) = 18.06 dB

[D] Sound-speed mismatch
True c = 1540 m/s, beamforming c = 1500 m/s
Correct-model axial peak = 29.990 mm
Wrong-model axial peak   = 29.210 mm

[E] Phase-aberration-like delay error
RMS channel delay error ~= 24.89 ns
Peak response change = -2.56 dB relative to ideal

Interpretation:
DAS is a strong baseline, but finite aperture, dynamic range and
propagation-model errors limit what fixed coherent summation can do.
