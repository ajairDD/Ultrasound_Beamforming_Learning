[7] TIMING CONVENTION - READ THIS BEFORE WRITING DAS
USTB channel_data.time(k) is defined as:
  initial_time + sample_index/fs + sequence(k).delay

Therefore a custom beamformer must choose ONE consistent convention:
  A) use channel_data.time(k), and do NOT add wave.delay again; OR
  B) use a common time axis based on initial_time, and include
     wave.delay explicitly in the Tx-delay equation.

Never do both, or wave.delay will be double counted.

How to read the figures:
  Spherical everywhere:
    physically intuitive away from focus, but off-axis focal
    pixels can suffer from the before/after-focus sign switch.
  Plane everywhere:
    removes that focal sign-switch problem, but ignores lateral
    wavefront curvature away from focus.
  Blended:
    combines the models with continuous radial weights.
    Off-axis delay jumps can remain if the spherical weight
    is nonzero at the focal plane. See the model diagnostic.
